How to get there — pick your moves

Nothing below is assumed by default. Switch on what you'd actually do; the hero updates.
This part is arithmetic — it can't go wrong. What can go wrong is whether the monthly amount actually happens. Returns may be 10% one decade and 14% the next; the moves above are what you control.

Why is the number so big?

  • You'll live off it for ~40 years, not 15. Retire at 50, plan to 90.
  • Lifestyle creep. You came to the city by train; you won't go back by train. The Seltos doesn't become an Alto at 50.
  • Healthcare and help cost more with age — and rise faster than everything else (we use 14%, not 6%).
  • Taxes don't retire. Withdrawals are taxed; we plan post-tax.
  • Every generation under-estimates by 4–6×. In 2006 people spending ₹30K/month said "₹1 Cr is enough"; the honest number was ~₹6 Cr. Your gut said .
