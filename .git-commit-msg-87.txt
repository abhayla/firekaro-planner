fix(fire): household tax is the sum of each earner's own tax, never one pooled filer (#87)

RCA: derive.ts built the household's annual income tax with ONE recommendRegime +
ONE computeTax over pooled income (salary + businessShare + otherTaxable -
rentalTaxDeduction) - a single-filer model. India taxes each adult separately, so
pooling pushed the second earner's whole income through the first earner's marginal
slabs and granted the household ONE basic exemption, ONE standard deduction and ONE
87A rebate instead of one each. Every one of those errors runs in the same direction:
too much tax -> too little annualSavings -> a FIRE date reported LATER and a
prescription HIGHER than the truth. Tier-0 honesty defect for the dual-income
salaried-accumulator persona.

Class: every household with >= 2 earning adults. Before: one pooled return. After:
annualTax = sum over earning adults of that adult's own tax on that adult's own income
(own salary CTC; own/Joint-split other income and business share; unowned rows to the
anchor adult; rental collapse split by the same complementary share), each adult's own
deductions via deductionsForMember (#204) and each adult electing their own regime.
Single-earner households byte-identical.
